How does this exchange work?

Pick a position on StrawPoll: SF (Strongly For), F (For), Abstain, A (Against), or SA (Strongly Against).

Each price is that position's share of votes, expressed in cents on a dollar. Prices round to a total of $$1.00. An empty poll has no price yet. These are opinions, not probabilities, trades, or official WG21 votes.

StrawPoll collects the votes and limits each poll to one vote per IP address. Shared networks share that limit; an IP address is not a person. Voting on StrawPoll requires JavaScript.

With JavaScript enabled, prices and vote counts refresh every minute. Without it, this page shows the latest published snapshot.

The graph shows recorded price snapshots. New history is published roughly every five minutes, starting with this site's first observation. It does not reconstruct earlier votes.

Not affiliated with ISO or WG21. Contracts settle to absolutely nothing. Snapshot $updated_display
